Antimatter is a free, open-source team chat you host yourself: channels and threads, voice rooms and calls, notes, boards and AI, all on your own server.

- Talk in channels, threads, group and direct messages
- Find anything with full-text search, filters and #hashtags
- Share images, video, audio and files
- Join calls and voice rooms from your phone
- Connect your own systems with webhooks, slash commands and integrations

To use this app, you need the address of an Antimatter server. To host your own, see https://antimatter.chat/install.html

Terms of use: https://antimatter.chat/terms.html

Antimatter is based on Mattermost. The source of this app is at https://github.com/AntimatterChat/mobile-app
