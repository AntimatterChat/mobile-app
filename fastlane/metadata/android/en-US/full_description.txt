Antimatter is a free, open-source team chat you host yourself: channels and threads, voice rooms and calls, notes, boards and AI, all on your own server.

- Talk in channels, threads, group and direct messages
- Share and view images, video and files
- Join calls and voice rooms from your phone
- Connect your own systems with webhooks, slash commands and integrations

To use this app, you need the address of an Antimatter server.
